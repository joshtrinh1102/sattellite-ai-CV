## COVID vs normal

Answers *"do ships during covid have less than ships during normal times"* from requirements.md — but the pandemic has to be split in two, because at San Pedro Bay it moved ship counts in opposite directions. Spring 2020 was a demand collapse; from late 2020 the import surge produced the anchorage backlog. Averaging them together answers the question wrongly rather than weakly.

| period | dates | mean ships/km2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| pre_covid | 15 | 0.142 |
| covid_collapse | 0 | - |
| covid_backlog | 23 | 0.148 |
| post | 38 | 0.125 |

No usable dates fall in: **covid_collapse**. This is the haze screen biting, not an absence of satellite passes — March to August over San Pedro Bay is marine-layer season, and the 2020 demand-collapse window sits inside it. The collapse phase therefore cannot be measured here at all, which is a gap in the answer to the requirement's COVID question rather than a null result for it.

**covid_backlog** vs pre-COVID + post-2022 baseline: 0.148 vs 0.130 ships/km2, **+14.2%** (bootstrap 95% CI [+0.001, +0.037], Mann-Whitney p = 0.0407, n=23 vs 53). The interval excludes zero, so the shift is detectable at this sample size.

## Exploratory ranking (too few dates for the model)

Only **23** capture dates have full news coverage, below the 60 the elastic net needs. Each news dimension is instead ranked by its partial Spearman correlation with ship density, controlling for `days_to_lny`, `month_sin`, `month_cos`, `time_trend`, with a bootstrap 95% interval and a Benjamini-Hochberg correction across the dimensions. Weather is not controlled for at this size.

Read this as a first look, not a ranking of effects: it is univariate, so it cannot separate two dimensions that rise together, and with this few dates the intervals are wide. ## What this does not establish

- **Association, not causation.** One port, a handful of disruption episodes, and no counterfactual.
- **Topic labels are keyword rules**, not the trained classifier 3_ranking/plan-topics.md specifies, and the 300-title gold set does not exist yet. Category precision is unmeasured and bounds everything downstream.
- **Ship counts carry detector error.** Held-out precision and recall are reported in `ship_detector_s2.report.json`; that uncertainty is not propagated into the intervals above.
- **Clear-sky dates only.** Scenes under the marine layer are discarded, which thins summer months and is not a random missingness pattern.
